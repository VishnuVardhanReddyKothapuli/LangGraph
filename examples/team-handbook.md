# Acorn Studio · Team handbook

## Learning policy L-104
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 30,000. The budget can be
used for books, courses, and professional conferences. Manager approval is
required before making a purchase. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Remote work policy R-202
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Team members should
be available from 11:00 to 15:00 India Standard Time for collaboration.

## Equipment requests
Submit equipment requests to the operations team. Include the item, estimated
cost, and reason for the request. Standard requests are reviewed within five
business days.
